# Brag Plan: Daedalus (high motion cut)

## What stays
The 30 second narrated cut. Seven scenes, seven local Kokoro voice lines, plain English, no em dashes, no hairlines, no caption labels, no god framing. Fully Geist Sans. Serious and cool. OpenStore paper base with teal as the main highlight.

## What changes
- Faster motion. Entries and exits are 0.2 to 0.4 seconds.
- Sliding mask reveals on text. Words arrive from below or the side, then hold.
- Every scene change uses a different move: push left, slide up, bottom up wipe, slide right, top down wipe, diagonal shift, scale in.
- Slightly more color. Teal stays primary. A blue and an amber appear on selected words, and a few panels use light tints of those colors. The frame stays mostly paper and ink.
- Staggered group entries for the eight jobs and the four check steps.

## Color
- Paper #f6f5f2, card #edece7, ink #1b1b1f, muted #55585f
- Teal #0f6b5c primary
- Blue #1a6491 and amber #a35b17 as small supporting accents
- Light tints: teal #e4efeb, blue #e6eef4, amber #f5ece0

## Scenes and motion
1. Hook. Two lines slide up in masks. Scene pushes left.
2. Message. Scene slides in from right. Lines slide up. Card slides up. Scene slides up out.
3. Jobs. Bottom up wipe. Head drops in. Eight names stagger from alternating sides. Scene scales down out.
4. Memory. Scene slides in from left. Panels stagger in from left with tinted fills. Scene slides right out.
5. Checks. Top down wipe. Steps stagger up. Scene scales up out.
6. Privacy. Scene slides in from right. Laptop slides in from left, text slides up in masks. Scene slides down out.
7. Name. Scale in. Tag and call to action slide up. Hold.

## Narration
Same seven lines and timings as the previous cut. Voice am_michael, generated locally. Music ducked under the voice, back up at the end. Soft clicks on the message, jobs, check step, and call to action.

## Audio boundary
No .env, no OpenRouter key, no external API, no spend. Local TTS only.

## Share copy
Daedalus runs on your laptop. You send one message and it does the work. Nothing private leaves the machine.
